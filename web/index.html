How it works

RSVP (rapid serial visual presentation) shows one word at a time in a fixed spot, so your eyes skip the jumps they normally make between words.

  • Red fixation letter. Each word's optimal recognition point, slightly left of centre, is drawn in red and pinned to the tick marks.
  • Natural pacing. Sentence ends get 2× time, commas 1.5×, paragraph breaks 2.5×. Long words and numbers get a little extra.
  • PDF cleanup. Running headers and footers and page numbers are removed, words split across lines are rejoined, and you can drop the references section, citations like [12], links and emails.
  • Speed ramp. Start slow and finish fast (say 300 → 900 wpm); the counter in the corner shows the current speed.
  • In your browser. pdf.js reads the PDF, a canvas draws each frame, and the browser's own H.264 encoder (WebCodecs) writes the MP4. The same engine also exists as a Python command-line tool.

Comprehension drops at very high speeds, so this works best for skimming, review and practice.
